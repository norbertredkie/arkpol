# ARKPOL — Copy Assessment & Rewrite
**Reviewer's lens:** CCO, Saatchi & Saatchi · **Date:** 2026-06-17 · **Scope:** full homepage copy

> Saatchi principle applied: people don't buy a mover, they buy *the feeling that nothing
> will go wrong*. Sell certainty (the emotional benefit), prove it with specifics, and never
> let internal/strategy language leak onto the page.

---

## Verdict (before)

**Strengths:** strong spine ("Move With Certainty"), some genuinely good lines ("Trusted
where it cannot go wrong", "Not a mover. A logistics standard."), honest geography after
the last pass, solid FAQ depth for SEO.

**Six problems worth fixing:**

1. **Feature-led, not benefit-led.** Service tiles read like a spec sheet ("containerised sea
   freight"). The buyer wants the *outcome* — it arrives intact, on time, no surprises.
2. **Internal language on a customer page.** "Guides that get cited" / "the answer when buyers
   (and AI) ask" is *our* SEO strategy, not *their* benefit. Cardinal sin. Cut it.
3. **Credential imprecise.** "IAM Member" undersells it. The badge is **IAMTrusted Moving
   Company** — a vetted, real-time-validated status. Name it correctly; it's a trust asset.
4. **Unverified specifics.** "25+ EU markets" is a number we can't stand behind. Specifics build
   trust only when true — replace with a claim that's defensible.
5. **Voice drift.** Hero/Statement are premium and human; tiles and some headings go flat and
   functional. One voice: confident, precise, quietly human.
6. **Flat section headings.** "International moving, explained." and "Guides that get cited."
   don't earn their scroll. Headings should reward attention.

## Voice guide (for all future copy)

- **Confident, never boastful.** State the fact, let it land. ("Trusted where it cannot go wrong.")
- **Specific beats adjective.** "Our own crews from two warehouses" > "high-quality service."
- **Benefit first, feature second.** Lead with what the client gets; back it with how.
- **Human, not corporate.** Short sentences. Plain words. A little warmth.
- **Honest.** Every claim defensible. No borrowed authority, no unverified numbers.
