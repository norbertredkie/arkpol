---

## VERSION 1 — Rationale

The three-layer structure works because:

- **H1** ("Move With Certainty") = emotional promise. Short, confident, zero wasted words.
- **Sub-headline** = four hard facts that back the promise up. Not fluffy — "Own EU Crews", "NATO", "30 years", "Worldwide" are all verifiable.
- **Supporting line** = operational specificity that converts skeptics. Answers "how?" without forcing them to dig.

**"Own EU Crews"** in the stat strip is the sleeper hit — most competitors subcontract local crews at destination (where quality falls apart). Arkpol not doing that is a concrete, verifiable trust signal that is scannable within 2 seconds of landing.

---

## Principles for future iterations

1. **H1 must be a promise, not a description.** "We are The Movers" = description. "Move With Certainty" = promise.
2. **Sub-headline must have at least one fact that sounds impossible.** "Trusted by NATO" makes people stop.
3. **CTAs:** quote CTA always primary (gold/filled) — it's the money action.
4. **Stat strip:** include "Own EU Crews" always — no competitor can say it.
